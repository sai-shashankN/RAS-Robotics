# Design and research direction

Two independently driven wheels sit on either side of a cylindrical body. Differential wheel motion supplies ordinary steering. The body carries the primary electronics and proposed camera/sensor windows;it still pitches and turns with the robot.

Alpha has a fixed front shoulder,elbow,wrist pitch and two articulated curling fingers. Alpha-A and Alpha-B compare front/back and left/right pinch axes. Closure can translate an object;sloped finger joints can affect release. Stable support transfer,withdrawal and final placement must be tested rather than inferred from the commanded wrist pose.

Beta adds a hollow rotating sleeve that carries the arm,and claw roll to orient the pinch axis. Those are distinct joints. A360degreeorientation objective does not establish endless rotation,cable routing,collision freedom or useful reach in every orientation.

X explores extra arm orientation and using an empty hand for support,lifting,recovery or low-ledge assistance. A two-finger manipulation hand has not been qualified to support the robot. Its footprint,load path,contact design and tipping/sliding behavior need a separate design and test programme.

Hardware assembly is deferred until the software studies and component assumptions are sufficiently developed. Sensor types and suppliers remain provisional;no procurement is implied by a simulation model.
